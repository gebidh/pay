How to Calculate Reverse Invoice Fees?

When invoicing a client as a freelancer or merchant, payment processing fees are deducted from the Gross Amount charged. If you ask for $100 without accounting for fees, Stripe or PayPal will deduct their percentage, leaving you with less than your target payout.

Reverse Fee Formula:
Gross Invoice = (Target Net + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Percentage Rate)

Example: To receive exactly $100.00 via Stripe (2.9% + $0.30), you must invoice $103.30.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard Stripe fee in 2026?

Stripe's standard fee for domestic US card processing is 2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge. International cards incur an extra 1.5% fee.

What is the standard PayPal merchant fee in 2026?

PayPal Checkout's standard domestic commercial transaction rate is 3.49% + $0.49 per transaction.

Are payment processing fees tax deductible?

Yes. In most countries, payment processing fees charged by merchant services are considered legitimate business expenses and can be deducted during tax filing.
