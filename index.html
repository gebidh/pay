Frequently Asked Questions

How does reverse fee calculation work?

When invoicing clients, payment processors take a fee percentage from the total charge, not the net amount. To receive an exact amount (e.g. $100), you must use the formula: Gross = (Target Net + Fixed Fee) / (1 - Percentage Rate).

Are Stripe and PayPal fees tax deductible?

Yes, in most jurisdictions, merchant processing fees are considered ordinary business expenses and can be deducted during tax filing.
